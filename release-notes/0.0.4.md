## zh-CN

- 新增 `date` 属性维度：`2020-04-14`、`2026.03.29`、`2020/04/14`、`2020年4月14日` 等日期统一输出为 `YYYY-MM-DD`，不再残留为集数或范围；日期中的年份仍照常作为 `year` 属性给出。
- 修正四位数年份被当作集数范围边界：`Ranma ½ 2024 - 27` 不再误读为范围 `2024-27`，末尾的集数正常识别；`1080-1085`、`01 ~ 20` 等真实范围不受影响。
- 修正方括号内的日期被读成月-日范围：`[2020-04-14]` 不再产生多余的集数范围。

## en

- Adds a `date` attribute dimension: `2020-04-14`, `2026.03.29` and `2020年4月14日` are reported as `YYYY-MM-DD` instead of leaking into an episode range; the year is still reported as `year`.
- Fixes a four-digit year being read as an episode-range bound: `Ranma ½ 2024 - 27` no longer yields the range `2024-27`; the trailing episode parses, and real ranges like `1080-1085` are unchanged.
- Fixes a bracketed date being read as a leftover month–day range: `[2020-04-14]` no longer yields a stray episode range.
